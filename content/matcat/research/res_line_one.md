<p>
Some compounds exhibit exotic chemical bonding features which might lead to extraordinary
chemical properties, including high stability or unexpected geometries. We study such
compounds utilizing a variety of state-of-the-art computational methods. Our goal is to
elucidate the mechanisms driving their behavior and leverage this understanding to design
novel materials.
