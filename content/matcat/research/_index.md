<section class="research-lines-intro">
<p>
MatCat Laboratory operates within the Donostia Kimika Teorikoa research group and
specializes in theoretical and computational approaches to catalysis and materials. Under
the leadership of Dr. Elisa Jiménez-Izal and Prof. Jose M. Mercero, the lab aims to
advance fundamental understanding of catalytic processes at surfaces, electrochemical
interfaces and materials with uncomom chemical bonding through state-of-the-art electronic
structure methods and multiscale modeling.
</p>
<p>
The core research lines of the laboratory are outlined in the cards below.
</p>
</section>

<h2>Exotic Matter</h2>
<p>
Electronic structure and bonding in unconventional materials, low-dimensional systems and

<h2>Metal Nanoclusters on Surfaces</h2>
<p>

<h2>Heterogeneous Catalysis and Electrocatalysis</h2>
<p>
