Research Groups

iSoM

iSoM-KT

Computational soft matter, ionic liquids, interfaces and multiscale modelling of complex molecular environments, led by Elixabete Rezabal.

MatCat

MatCat-KT

Theoretical and computational catalysis, electrochemical interfaces, nanostructures on surfaces and materials design, co-led by Elisa Jimenez-Izal and Jose M. Mercero.

Bio

Bio-KT

Electronic structure and modelling strategies aimed at biologically relevant molecules, processes and interactions, co-led by Xabier Lopez and David de Sancho.

Pol

Pol-KT

Polymer theory and simulation, from molecular-scale structure to functional soft materials and responsive systems, co-led by Jon Mattin Matxain and Fernando Ruiperez.

NOFT-KT

Natural orbital functional theory, methodological development and applications in quantum chemistry, led by Mario Piris.

MolEleS-KT

Molecular electronics and spectroscopic properties of functional systems at the interface between chemistry and materials science, led by David Casanova.

QCD-KT

Conceptual and methodological developments in quantum chemistry, bonding analysis and electronic structure descriptors, led by Eduard Matito.

MoMag

MoMag-KT

Modelling magnetism and molecular magnetic materials, with emphasis on structure-property relationships, led by Dani Reta.
