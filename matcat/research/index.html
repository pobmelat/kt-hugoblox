Research

MatCat Laboratory operates within the Donostia Kimika Teorikoa research group and specializes in theoretical and computational approaches to catalysis and materials. Under the leadership of Dr. Elisa Jiménez-Izal and Prof. Jose M. Mercero, the lab aims to advance fundamental understanding of catalytic processes at surfaces, electrochemical interfaces and materials with uncomom chemical bonding through state-of-the-art electronic structure methods and multiscale modeling.

The core research lines of the laboratory are outlined in the cards below.

Research Line 01

Exotic Matter

Electronic structure and bonding in unconventional materials, low-dimensional systems and chemically unusual environments where standard intuition is often insufficient.

Open research line

Research Line 02

Metal Nanoclusters on Surfaces

Supported nanoclusters, nanoalloys and interfacial effects on surfaces, with emphasis on stability, adsorption, restructuring and catalytic activity.

Open research line

Research Line 03

Heterogeneous Catalysis and Electrocatalysis

Reaction mechanisms at surfaces and electrochemical interfaces, from active-site analysis to catalyst optimization under realistic working conditions.

Open research line
